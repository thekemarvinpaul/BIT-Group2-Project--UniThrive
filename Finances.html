// Smart Money Tips

Student Finance Tips

01
The 50/30/20 Rule for Students

Allocate 50% to needs (food, housing, transport), 30% to wants (social, entertainment), and 20% to savings. Adjust for your bursary size.

02
Cook More, Spend Less

Preparing even 3 meals a week instead of buying can save UGX 30,000–60,000 monthly. Organize group cooking with housemates.

03
Use the Marketplace First

Before buying anything new, check the student marketplace. Textbooks, calculators, and equipment often sell for 50–70% less.

04
Track Every Transaction

Even UGX 500 entries matter. Students who track daily often save 20% more by becoming aware of small habitual spending.

05
Share Rides & Data

Splitting transport with 3 others saves UGX 12,000+ monthly. Group data bundle purchases are often 30% cheaper per person.

06
Set Micro-Goals

Instead of "save money", set specific targets: "Save UGX 50,000 by end of month." Specific goals are 3× more likely to be achieved.
